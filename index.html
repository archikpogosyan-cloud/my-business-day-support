Frequently Asked Questions

Is My Business Day free?

Yes. My Business Day offers a free plan with limits on business records. Pro subscriptions provide additional capacity.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open My Business Day, go to the Pro subscription screen, and select "Restore Purchases".

How can I report a problem?

Email our support address with a description of the issue and your device type. Please do not send passwords or verification codes.

Can I request account or data deletion?

Yes. Contact us by email with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will provide instructions for your request.
