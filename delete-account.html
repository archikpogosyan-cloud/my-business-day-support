This is the official account deletion page for My Business Day, the business management app developed by Artur Pohosian.

Request account deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated data even if you no longer have access to the app.

  1. Email mybusinessday.support@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address associated with your My Business Day account.
  3. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request. Never send passwords or verification codes.
Request Account Deletion

Delete from the app

In app versions that support account deletion, sign in, open Settings, choose Delete Account and confirm. If the option is unavailable in your installed version, use the email request above.

What data is deleted?

Deleting your account removes its authentication profile and associated company-owned business records, including clients, orders, tasks, appointments, financial records, vehicles and rentals. Deletion is irreversible.

Companies shared with other members may require additional steps before deletion. Certain information may be retained where legally required or necessary for security, compliance and handling support requests. App stores and payment processors manage their own transaction records.

Important: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel subscriptions separately through your store's subscription settings.
