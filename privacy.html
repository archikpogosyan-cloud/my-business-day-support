MY BUSINESS DAY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

My Business Day is a business management application developed by Artur Pohosian. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use My Business Day on iOS or Android.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the application, information may include:

  • Account information, such as an email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Business information that you enter, including client records, orders, tasks, appointments, financial transactions and notes.
  • Subscription information, including subscription status, purchase identifiers and entitlement information.
  • Technical information required for authentication, synchronization, security and app functionality.
  • Information you voluntarily send when contacting support.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

  • Provide and operate the application.
  • Authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • Save and synchronize business records.
  • Manage business features, tasks and reminders.
  • Verify subscriptions and provide Pro features.
  • Protect accounts and respond to support requests.

3. Local Storage and Cloud Synchronization

My Business Day uses local device storage for app functionality. When synchronization is enabled, supported business records and account-related data are processed using Supabase cloud services.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have permission to enter personal information about their clients, employees or other individuals.

4. Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party infrastructure and services, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database services and synchronization.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and purchase entitlement verification.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — processing purchases and subscriptions through the relevant platform.

These providers may process information required to deliver their respective services, subject to their applicable privacy policies.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

My Business Day offers free and paid features. In-app subscriptions are processed through Apple or Google payment systems. The application uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status and access to Pro features.

We do not directly receive or store full payment card details from App Store or Google Play subscription transactions.

6. Notifications

The application may use device notifications to remind users about tasks and appointments. Notification permissions can be managed through device settings.

7. Data Security

My Business Day uses technical safeguards, including access controls and authentication, to help protect user information. Backup files may use encryption features. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as needed to provide app functionality, maintain account records, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve security or support issues.

Users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting: mybusinessday.support@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Requests may require verification of account ownership. Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and compliance purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

My Business Day is designed for business management and is not directed at children.

10. International Data Processing

Service providers may process or store information in countries other than the user's country of residence, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in app functionality, services or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact Us

For questions about privacy, personal information, or account deletion, contact:

mybusinessday.support@gmail.com
